Home, All Locked! Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026
한국어

Home, All Locked! ("the App", 나 홀로 문단속 in Korean) is a casual game where you lock the doors and windows of your house all night to keep intruders out. It is operated by Ricecookey ("we"). The App is built so that it does not collect your personal information on our servers.

At a glance

  • 🙅No sign-up or login. We never ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • 📱Stage progress, coins and gems, upgrades, cosmetics and settings are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us.
  • 📣Rewarded ads (Google AdMob) play only when you choose a start boost, a revive or double rewards. Google processes advertising identifiers.
  • 💳In-app purchases such as Remove Ads are handled by Google Play and the App Store. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • 🗑️Deleting the App deletes everything stored on your device.

2. Retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device is deleted when you delete the App.
  • Purchase records remain in your Google Play or App Store account under each store's policy.
  • We do not keep any of your personal information.

4. Ads and tracking

  • Rewarded ads play only when you tap a button that offers one (start with two doors locked, revive, double rewards). No ads play on their own between rounds.
  • With Remove Ads, you get those rewards instantly without watching an ad.
  • In regions that require consent, such as the EEA and UK, Google's consent form appears on first launch. You can play without consenting (non-personalized ads).
  • On iOS, the App asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. If you decline, the IDFA is not used and the game works the same. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Game data is never used for ads or shared with Google.

5. In-app purchases

  • Payments are handled only by the Google Play and App Store billing systems. We never receive or store card numbers or other payment details.
  • The App uses only the purchase confirmation provided by the store (product and purchase status) to deliver items.
  • Refunds follow each store's policy and process.

7. Your rights

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Security

  • Ad and payment traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
