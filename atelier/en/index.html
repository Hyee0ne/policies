Atelier of Starlight — Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026

An app where a child's drawing becomes real clothing. Because children use it, this page states plainly what we receive, what it is used for, and when it is deleted.

At a glance

  • No account, no sign-in. We never ask for a name, age, contact details or location.
  • Only the child's drawing is sent to our server, and only to create the clothing image.
  • No ads, no tracking, nothing sold to anyone.
  • Drawings and generated images are deleted automatically after 90 days.

1. What is sent to our server

Only when the clothing spell is cast. Nothing is sent while the child is simply drawing or playing.

We do not collect names, dates of birth, gender, contact details, addresses, location, contacts, photo library contents or advertising identifiers. A drawing carries no information about who drew it.

2. What stays on the device

Drawings in progress, finished outfits, story progress and mixed colours are stored on the device only. Deleting the app removes them. We never receive them.

3. Processors we use

  • Google Cloud — hosting and storage, in the Seoul (South Korea) region
  • Google Gemini API — generating the clothing image
  • OpenAI — generating the thank-you letter photo

We send only the drawing and a short English description, never anything identifying the sender. Both companies state that content sent through their paid APIs is not used to train their models, and we use the paid APIs only.

4. Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising. We do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), do not track activity across other apps or websites, and share nothing with advertising companies.

5. Children and guardians

The app is for all ages. We never ask a child for a name or an age, and we collect nothing that identifies a child.

A parent or guardian may ask us to delete a drawing by emailing the address below. Deleting the app clears everything on the device immediately; anything on the server is deleted within 90 days, or sooner on request.

6. Permissions the app asks for

  • Save to Photos — only when saving a finished picture. We never read the photo library.
  • Local network — used only to reach a development or test server. It is not used in normal play.

7. Keeping it safe

All traffic is encrypted with https. Stored drawings are reachable only by authorised staff and are removed automatically under the 90-day rule.

8. Changes to this policy

We update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes are also announced in the app.
