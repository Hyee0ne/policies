Brrrp Privacy Policy

Effective: July 29, 2026

Brrrp (the “App”) puts your privacy first. The App uses the camera to recognize your facial expression so you can turn off alarms, but never stores or transmits any face-related data. It uses the network to download expression-challenge content and to send anonymous statistics that help improve the service, but none of that includes your face or any personally identifying information.

At a glance

  • 📷Camera video and expression data are processed only on your device, in real time.
  • 🚫Face images, video, and expression data are never stored and never transmitted anywhere.
  • 🌐The App uses the network to fetch challenge content and send anonymous statistics — never your face or personal information.
  • 📊Only anonymous, non-identifying statistics are collected (no name, account, or ad identifier).
  • 🗑️Alarm and settings data are stored only on your device and are deleted when you remove the App.

2. Camera & Facial (Expression) Data

  • The App uses the front camera only on the alarm-dismissal challenge screen (copying an expression).
  • Expression recognition uses only Apple ARKit face tracking (blendShape coefficients) — numeric values indicating expression intensity, such as “mouth-corner raise 0.8.” They are used for real-time judging and then immediately discarded.
  • Camera video frames and face images never leave the screen, are not saved to any file, are not captured or recorded, and are never transmitted.
  • Expression data is used only for challenge judging, processed only in device memory, and the camera session ends when the challenge finishes.
  • This has nothing to do with Face ID or other device biometrics; the App does not create or use any biometric template.

3. Information the App Stores on Your Device

  • Alarm settings (time, repeat days, alarm sound, snooze settings, etc.)
  • App preferences (e.g., whether manual dismissal is allowed)

This information is stored only on your device without any sign-up or account, and is deleted when you remove the App.

4. Anonymous Statistics for Service Improvement

To improve challenge difficulty and usability, the App collects anonymous, non-identifying statistics.

  • Collected: a randomly generated anonymous install identifier, platform (iOS), the challenge/expression attempted, success or exit, the recognition score reached, and time taken.
  • Not collected: personally identifying information such as name, email, phone number, or location; face images, video, or raw expression data; advertising identifiers (IDFA).

These statistics do not identify any individual and are never combined with face data. The statistics are stored and processed by a third-party cloud service (Supabase Inc.).

5. Network & Content Delivery

The App downloads expression-challenge (pack and expression) content from a third-party cloud service (Supabase Inc.) to keep it up to date. This communication does not include any face or personally identifying information. The App uses no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK and no advertising identifier (IDFA).

6. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personally identifying information such as name, email, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Face images or video, biometric templates
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA), cross-app tracking data

7. Third Parties & Processing

The App does not sell personal information or provide it to third parties for advertising. It does use a cloud infrastructure service (Supabase Inc.) for content delivery and anonymous statistics — and even then, no face data or personally identifying information is shared.

8. Permissions

You can change permissions anytime in iOS Settings > Brrrp. If you deny the camera, you can dismiss alarms via manual dismissal instead of the expression challenge, when enabled in settings.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not collect personally identifying information from children.

10. Retention & Deletion

Information stored on your device (alarms and settings) is deleted immediately when you remove it in the App or delete the App. Anonymous statistics are retained, in a form that cannot identify any individual, solely to improve the service.

11. Your Rights

Because your face data and app settings exist only on your device, you have full control and can delete them entirely by removing them in the App or deleting the App. Anonymous statistics are not tied to any individual and therefore cannot be individually identified or deleted. If this policy changes, we will notify you through an app update and this page, and update the effective date accordingly.
