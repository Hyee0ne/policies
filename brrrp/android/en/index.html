Brrrp Privacy Policy (Android)

Effective 27 August 2026 · 한국어

Brrrp ("the app") puts your privacy first. The app uses the camera to read your facial expression so you can turn off an alarm, but no face-related data is ever stored or sent anywhere. The app uses the network to send anonymous usage statistics, to download challenge content, and to show ads. None of it carries any facial data.

At a glance

  • 📷Camera frames and expression data are processed only on your device, in real time.
  • 🚫Face images, video, and expression data are never saved and never transmitted.
  • 🌐The network is used only for anonymous statistics, downloading challenge content, and showing ads — never for face data.
  • 📊Only anonymous statistics that cannot identify you are collected (no name, no account).
  • 📣The Dashboard shows a banner ad, and unlocking a paid challenge plays a rewarded ad you choose to watch. The ad provider (Google AdMob) uses an advertising identifier.
  • 🗑️ Alarms and settings live only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Camera and facial expression data

  • The app uses the front camera only on the alarm-dismissal challenge screen (copying an expression).
  • Expression recognition uses only the blendshape coefficients from Google MediaPipe Face Landmarker. These are numeric intensity values — for example, "mouth corner raised: 0.8" — used for scoring in real time and discarded immediately afterwards.
  • Camera frames and images of your face never leave the screen, are never written to a file, are never captured or recorded, and are never transmitted.
  • Expression data is used solely to score the challenge, is held only in device memory, and the camera session ends when the challenge ends.
  • This has nothing to do with your device's biometric unlock (fingerprint or face unlock). The app does not create or use biometric templates. The recognition model ships inside the app and runs on the device; recognition itself uses no network.

3. What the app stores on your device

  • Alarm settings (time, repeat days, alarm sound, and so on)
  • Wake-up records (time, expression, and challenge for each success) and the badges derived from them
  • App preferences (for example, the challenge currently in progress)
  • Which challenges you have unlocked by watching an ad — kept on your device only, never sent to a server.

There is no sign-up and no account. This information is stored only on your device and is deleted entirely when you uninstall the app.

4. Anonymous statistics for improving the service

The app collects statistics that cannot identify you, in order to improve challenge difficulty and usability.

  • What is collected (in full): a randomly generated anonymous installation identifier, the platform (Android), which challenge and expression was attempted, whether it succeeded or was skipped, the recognition score reached, and how long it took
  • What is not collected: personally identifying information such as name, email, phone number, or location; face images, video, or raw expression data; device model or operating system version

The anonymous installation identifier is a random value the app generates for itself. It does not identify you or your device, it disappears when you uninstall the app, and reinstalling produces a different value. It is unrelated to the Advertising ID or to any hardware identifier.

These statistics do not identify any individual and are never combined with face data. They are stored and processed by a third-party cloud service, Supabase Inc.

5. Network use

The app uses the network for three things only — sending the anonymous statistics described in section 4, downloading challenge content, and showing the ads described in section 5-1. None of it carries any facial data.

Challenge content (the list of expressions, their wording, and their artwork) is checked for updates when you open the app, and is only ever downloaded. Nothing about you is sent with that request — it receives, it does not send.

The content also ships inside the app, so alarms and challenges keep working with no internet at all. If a download fails, the app quietly falls back to the content it already has.

5-1. Ads

There are two kinds of ads.

Banner ads appear only on the Dashboard screen. There are no ads on the alarm screen or on the expression challenge (mission) screen — nothing should get in the way of turning an alarm off.

Rewarded ads play only when you tap "Watch an ad to unlock" yourself, to open a paid challenge. They never appear on their own, and everything else in the app works whether or not you watch one. Watch it through and that challenge unlocks; that fact is recorded on your device only (section 3).

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses an advertising identifier along with device and app information to select ads and measure their performance. That processing follows Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • Face data is never used for ads. Expression recognition results are not passed to the ad provider.
  • In the EEA, the UK, and other regions where consent is required, a consent form appears before any ad is shown. If you decline, you will not see personalised ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

6. What we do not collect

  • Personally identifying information such as name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Face images or video, or biometric templates
  • Device specifications such as device model or operating system version

9. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personally identifying information from children.

10. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device (alarms, wake-up records, badges, settings) is destroyed as soon as you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Anonymous statistics are retained, in a form that cannot identify anyone, solely for improving the service.

11. Your rights

Face data and app settings exist only on your own device, so you have complete control: delete them in the app, or uninstall the app to remove them entirely. Anonymous statistics are not linked to any individual and therefore cannot be identified or deleted individually. For related enquiries, contact us at the address below.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will give notice through an app update and on this page, and we will update the effective date.
