Snap Clump Privacy Policy

Effective: October 10, 2026
Support 한국어

Snap Clump (착착: 자석블록퍼즐, "the app") is a casual game where you drag a magnet cluster to snap and pop same-color blocks. It is run by RiceCookey ("we"). The app works without an account.

At a glance

  • 🙅No sign-up or login. We don't ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • 📱Scores, coins, upgrades, skins, items, check-in and missions, workshop and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • 📊We receive anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) to improve the game. They don't include your name or contact details.
  • 📣You see rewarded ads when you choose them (Continue, 2x coins…) and occasional interstitial ads (Google AdMob) between runs.
  • 💳In-app purchases are handled by the App Store / Google Play. We never receive your card details.
  • 🗑️Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your device.

2. Retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Usage statistics are kept and deleted according to the Google Analytics data retention setting.
  • We don't keep any other personal information.

4. Ads and tracking

  • Rewarded ads play only when you tap to watch one. Interstitial ads appear occasionally after a run, never in your first two runs.
  • After your first run, Google's consent form (in regions that require it) and the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt may appear. You can play the same if you decline, and change it later in the app's Settings > Ad privacy or in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Google AdMob uses advertising IDs and device and app info to choose and measure ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Buying Remove Ads turns off interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay optional.

6. Your rights

  • Deleting the app removes the game data on your device. Purchases can be restored with the same store account in the app's Settings > Restore purchases.
  • Requests about statistics and ads can go to Google, and about payments to the store. For anything else, email us and we'll reply promptly.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14, and we don't knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Security

  • Data on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.
  • Statistics, ads and payments use encrypted connections (HTTPS).
